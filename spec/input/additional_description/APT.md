## APT

#### Why SDN?
In the past APT received its Microwave data from Mycom. Due to data often be missing, users were dissatisfied. Also it often was intransparent to users, why data was missing due to the complex chain from raw data to APT.  
With SDN data can be provisioned directly from the devices (via MWDI cache) through SDN applications to APT.  

#### APTP to PM app
APTP also provides data to APT through its exposed services.  
This data provisioning will be consolidated and moved to the DevicePerformanceManagementDataProcessor application.
